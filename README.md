# 🛡️ SafePay — Shop Confidently. Pay Securely.

SafePay is your trusted shopping companion that holds your payment in escrow until you receive exactly what you ordered. No more scams. No more guesswork. Just peace of mind.

---

## 🎯 Why SafePay?

Ever ordered something online and received the wrong item? Or worse — got scammed? SafePay protects your money by holding it safely until your product is verified and delivered.

- ✅ Only pay when the correct product is delivered.
- ✅ Record a quick video of the received item.
- ✅ Let our smart system verify it.
- ✅ Get your money back if it’s not what you ordered.

---

## 📱 How SafePay Works

1. You shop on any third-party vendor website.
2. At checkout, you select SafePay to complete the payment.
3. We hold your payment safely in escrow.
4. Once your product arrives, use our Scan & Verify tool to record a short video.
5. We match it with the original product listing.
6. ✅ If it’s a match, we release the payment to the seller.
7. ❌ If not, you get a refund. No questions asked.
